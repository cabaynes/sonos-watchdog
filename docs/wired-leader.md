# Wired speaker leads (optional)

**Status: being tested.** When a group contains a speaker on Ethernet, the group is steadier
with that speaker leading. Handing the lead over **while music plays** is unsafe — it
desynced a group in our test (see FINDINGS.md). Whether a handover **during a pause** is safe
is being tested; until then, the blueprint only logs whether each session's leader is wired
(the **Wired speakers** setting).

What you can do today without any automation: start your group from the wired room, so it
becomes the leader.
